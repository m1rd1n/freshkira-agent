## HOW TO USE THIS CALENDAR

This calendar is the agent's reference for:
- Timing campaign and content recommendations
- Structuring pricing adjustment windows
- Flagging upcoming sale events in any output
- Advising FreshKira on when to start pre-sale preparation

**Rule for the agent:** Any pricing or campaign recommendation must reference the next upcoming sale event within 45 days. Always advise FreshKira to prepare content and pricing 3–4 weeks before the event.

### 🔴 11.11 — Singles' Day Mega Sale
| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 11 November (annual) |
| **Platforms** | Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada (all run simultaneously) |
| **Traffic Impact** | Highest single-day GMV of the year — typically 5–10x normal daily sales |
| **Malaysian Consumer Behaviour** | Cart loading starts 2–3 weeks before; impulse buys peak on the day; voucher hunting is extreme |

| **Discount Depth** | 20–30% recommended (max 30% to maintain margin after platform fees); bundles outperform single SKU discounts |

| **freshkira.com.my** | Run exclusive website bundle at additional 5% off vs. platform price to drive direct traffic |
| **FreshKira Strategy** | Lead with FK-CLN-006 (cleanser, entry SKU) as traffic driver. Bundle FK-TON-001 + FK-SER-002 as "Glow & Repair Duo" at 20% off. Run TikTok Shop Live on the evening of 11 Nov. 10 days of countdown content. FKP double points on website purchases during 11.11 window. |
| **Content Themes** | "Our biggest sale of the year," countdown timers, before/after transformations, ingredient reveals, live product demos |
| **Key Hashtags** | #1111Sale #ShopeeMyMega #TikTokShopMY #LazadaMY #FreshKira1111 #GlowUpSale |

## PART 6 — TIKTOK SHOP MALAYSIA MECHANICS

| Mechanic | How It Works | FreshKira Application |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok Affiliate Programme** | Creators promote products for 5–15% commission | Onboard 10–20 micro-affiliates (10K–100K followers) in skincare niche; prioritise halal and Malaysian skincare communities |
| **TikTok Shop Live** | Sell directly during live stream; products shown in on-screen widget | Target Tuesday and Thursday 8–10 PM live slots; FK-MST-010 mist ideal for live demo (spray on camera) |
| **TikTok Shop Flash Deals** | Time-limited discounts featured on Shop tab | Participate in platform-curated flash deal windows for algorithm visibility |
| **LIVE Vouchers** | Vouchers redeemable only during a specific live session | "This voucher expires when we go offline" — urgency mechanic |
| **Shoppable Videos** | Products tagged directly in non-live TikTok videos | ALL FreshKira product videos must have TikTok Shop product tags enabled |
| **30-Day Return Window (TikTok Shop Mall)** | Customers have 30 days after delivery for return/refund on Mall listings | FreshKira must respond to return requests within 1 working day per TikTok Shop policy |
| **Change of Mind Returns** | Buyers can return most items; platform bears return shipping | FreshKira's own policy: unopened products within platform return window; opened products not eligible unless defect |

## PART 7 — RETURNS POLICY CONTEXT BY PLATFORM

| Platform | Return Window | Shipping Fee (Defect/Wrong Item) | Shipping Fee (Change of Mind) | FreshKira Response SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok Shop Mall** | 30 days from delivery | Seller bears cost | Platform bears cost | 1 working day |

**Agent rule:** Never confirm a specific refund outcome in a public reply. Always direct: "Please DM us and our team will guide you through the process." For TikTok Shop and Shopee, remind customers to open a return request through the platform app first.
