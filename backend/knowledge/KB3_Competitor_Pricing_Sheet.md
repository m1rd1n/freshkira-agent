## PRICING STRATEGY RULES FOR AGENT

1. FreshKira is NOT a budget brand. Never recommend pricing below Luminos Co.
2. FreshKira's reference ceiling is Skintific — pricing should stay below Skintific to win on value.
3. When recommending price changes, always factor in platform commission (TikTok 8% + potential 10% affiliate = 18% off retail before COGS).
4. Bundle pricing should always show a minimum 10% saving vs. individual SKU total.
5. During 11.11 and 12.12, discount depth should not exceed 30% — below this risks margin collapse given platform fees.
6. JAKIM halal and KKM registration are free differentiators — always include in value justification when competing on price.
